These are the SIZE-BAND edges used to stratify the training negative
draw. They are recomputed here by the same expression the draw uses,
from the same input under the same filter, and verified against the
committed per-cell counts before being written. See the VERIFICATION
section below.

They are STRATIFICATION bands. They are not the benchmark-derived
REPORTING bands, per tools/draw_negatives.py:30-31.

UNITS
  Normalised area. Dimensionless fraction of frame area, in [0, 1].
  The binned quantity is the area_frac column of proposals.csv, written
  at tools/extract_proposals.py:309 as the connected-component blob
  area in pixels divided by the frame area (fa = fw * fh,
  tools/extract_proposals.py:244).
  NOT equivalent side. No equivalent-side quantity enters the draw.

EXPRESSION, tools/draw_negatives.py:109-112 verbatim
  edges = np.percentile(pos_area, np.linspace(0, 100, n_bands + 1))
  inner = edges[1:-1]
  pos_band = np.digitize(pos_area, inner, right=False)
  neg_band = np.digitize(neg_area, inner, right=False)

BAND MEMBERSHIP
  np.digitize(..., right=False) puts a value v in band b where
  inner[b-1] <= v < inner[b]. Left-closed, right-open. Band 0 is
  v < inner[0] and band 9 is v >= inner[8].
  band 0 holds 1,334 positives, band 9 holds 1,336.

THE OUTER EDGES AGAINST THE RULED ANCHORS
  Two independent cross-checks, recorded as observations. Both fall out
  of the recomputation above and neither is hardcoded here.

  edges[0], the observed minimum, is 7.234e-06.
  configs/ch4.yaml:108 rules proposals.min_area_frac = 7.23e-06,
  the extraction floor, applied at tools/extract_proposals.py:246 as
  lo = min_frac * fa. The observed minimum sits just above that floor,
  at 1.000553 times it, which is what a floor applied
  at extraction produces.

  edges[10], the observed maximum, is 0.016628569,
  which is 1.6629e-02 to five significant figures.
  configs/ch4.yaml:110-111 records a measured anchor of 1.6629e-02, the
  "largest cover of a box at coverage >= 0.5". A proposal is positive
  exactly when its coverage reaches proposals.positive_coverage = 0.5
  (configs/ch4.yaml:114), so the largest positive area fraction and
  that anchor are the same quantity. They agree.
  This corroborates the anchor from which ruled.proposals.max_area_frac
  was derived. It is a cross-check, not a new measurement, and it does
  not revisit the max_area_frac ruling.
