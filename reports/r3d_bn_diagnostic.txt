CORRECTION APPENDED 27 AUGUST 2026. The text above is left unaltered.

The three subset AP values quoted above as "the matrix runs", 0.0084, 0.0051
and 0.0104, together with the losses 0.576, 0.581 and 0.581, have been resolved
against the run artefacts. Each is a single epoch-1 measurement taken from a
run that was then terminated by torch.OutOfMemoryError inside loss.backward()
during epoch 2. No one of the three completed a second epoch, stopped on
patience, or reached the epoch ceiling. They are therefore not matrix outcomes
and they are not evidence of a convergence failure in either direction.

The surviving records are preserved in runs_terminated/, the seed 1 epochs.csv
and checkpoint having been overwritten before the copy was taken.

The loss comparison reported above is unaffected by this correction. The frozen,
unfrozen and reset losses of 0.5775, 0.2491 and 0.2491 were measured by
tools/diagnose_r3d_bn.py in its own single-epoch run and do not depend on the
three quoted figures. The READING stated above stands as written.
